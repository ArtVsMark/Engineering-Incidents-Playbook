# A refusal to build a mechanism, measured and chosen, is neither a debt nor "impossible", and it has its own word

**Area.** contracts, metrics

**Tier.** 3 — gates and processes

**The rule.** The vocabulary of "can a machine hold this" distinguishes four
states, not three. The fourth: the machine half **exists and is measured**, and
building it was **refused**, because the signal would fire on what a project
decision explicitly allows — not only as a block but as a warning too. Such an
answer is not `no`: the half is computed by a command. It is not `not-yet`:
nobody intends to build it, and the debt would be inflated. It is not
`conditional`: the subject exists, and the signal would turn red, not stay
green. The word `refused` is legitimate only with a measurement in
`machine_half` and only after [051](051-warn-on-likely-block-on-certain.md):
first the signal is lowered to a warning, and only refusing the warning is a
refusal.

**Portable beyond Claude Code.** yes — the subject exists in any registry that
counts the share of machine-held compliance: a measured refusal added to the
debt skews the share for everyone who reads it.

## The incident

**THE NEIGHBOUR'S MEASUREMENT, WHICH THE PROPOSAL CAME FROM** (the mechanisms
project). On 13.09, with the catalogue's vocabulary at two words, an analysis
produced a case that fit neither: the half is computed and verified by a
measurement, yet it does not become a mechanism. The neighbour introduced its
own word, `measured-refusal`, and eight answers received it. On 17.09 the
contract rose to 1.5, the vocabulary became three words, and all eight were
re-read. **The re-read refuted the word on seven out of eight:** six meant
`no`, one meant `conditional`. One remained, 133: a change splits into
connected components along its branch commits, six of the last 25 merged ones
split — the signal catches a real mixing of subjects. It did not become a gate
because a project decision explicitly allows carrying a tail of small edits in
one change, and the signal would fire on every fourth pass. It was recorded
with the least false word, `not-yet`, and the project's debt has been inflated
by one ever since.

**HERE THE SAME SUBJECT IS SOLVED DIFFERENTLY — AND THAT IS THE WORD'S
BOUNDARY.** Our rule 133 is held by `scripts/check_overlap.py`: the file overlap
is printed into the run summary and does not hold the merge. A signal that also
fires on the legitimate is lowered to a warning under 051 and counts as a
mechanism. The re-read on 25.09 over the 55 answers no machine holds (18 `no`,
27 `not-yet`, 10 `conditional`) picked two candidates for the new word by
searching reasons and measurements, and neither fit on a full reading. 042 —
the vocabulary was refused on a measurement, but its second half, a canonical
part of the release section, awaits the owner's decision: that is a debt. 028
waits for a subject: that is a condition. The catalogue has zero `refused`
answers today.

## Why

**A measured refusal is the result of an analysis, not unfinished work.** Added
to the debt, it inflates the debt exactly where the analysis went deepest: the
more carefully a project measured and refused, the worse its count looks. Added
to "impossible", it lies about the tree: the half is computed by a command.

**Three words force a lie in one of three directions.** The neighbour went
through it twice: with a two-word scale the refusal stood as "impossible", with
a three-word scale as "not built". Both records were false, and false in
different directions.

**A word without a measurement becomes a catch-all.** Of the eight answers the
neighbour once marked as refusals, one survived the re-read. That is why the
measurement is mandatory, and why the word comes after 051: most "refusals" are
really either a warning nobody built or a half that does not exist.

**This is not [182](182-an-unmechanisable-answer-is-split-in-two.md).** 182
requires splitting the requirement to find the machine half. Here it has been
found and measured — and the question is no longer whether it exists but how
to name the decision not to build it.

## In practice

- 051 first: a signal that fires on the legitimate is lowered to a warning, and
  the warning counts as a mechanism. `refused` is for when that was refused too;
- `machine_half` names the measurement: what a command computes and how many
  times the signal would fire on the legitimate. `why` names the project
  decision the signal would violate;
- in the count a refusal stands as its own number, apart from "impossible",
  "not built" and "conditional", and it does not go into the tier-0 debt;
- the decision is revisited when the permitting project decision changes: the
  refusal is held by it, not by itself.

## Where it applies

**Works** for registries of answers to a rulebook where the share of
machine-held compliance is counted: a compliance map, a project's answer to
the catalogue.

**Does not work** where the signal can be lowered to a warning and nobody
refused that: it is a mechanism under 051, not a refusal. **Does not work**
either without a measurement: a refusal with no number behind it is
indistinguishable from "never looked".

**Sign of a violation:** a `not-yet` or `no` answer whose `why` describes a
working half and the decision not to build it.
