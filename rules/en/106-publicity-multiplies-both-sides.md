# Publicity multiplies both the good and the bad — do the real run first

**The rule.** A product is not shown widely until it has been through a **real**
scenario end to end. Promotion does not create quality; it magnifies both quality
and its absence.

## The incident

The tool is ready, the front page is built, the documentation is bilingual, there
are over five thousand checks. The natural next step is to talk about it where the
audience lives.

The owner's decision was to **wait**, and it is recorded plainly: first go through
a real course on the tool end to end and confirm that its verdicts agree with the
platform. The reason is stated in one sentence: promotion multiplies both the good
and the bad.

The "do the verdicts agree" check is not abstract. The worst defect of a checking
tool is accepting an incorrect solution: the person believes the task is done, the
platform does not accept it, and trust in the tool is gone. Such a defect is found
neither by tests nor by a code audit — only by a real run over real tasks.

## Why

Publicity is a **multiplier**, not an addend. It increases the number of people
who meet the product and does not change what they meet. With a hidden defect the
multiplier works against you: the same defect reaches everyone who arrives, all at
once.

Second: **the first impression is not recoverable**. Somebody who left because of
a breakage does not come back to see whether it was fixed. So the cost of
premature publicity is not paid once but subtracted from the entire future
audience.

Third, on the order of checks: what is needed before publicity is not "one more
audit" but **a run through a real scenario from beginning to end**. Audits and
tests check nodes; publicity exposes the chain, and a chain is verified only as a
whole.

## In practice

- the condition for publicity is phrased **observably**: not "when it is ready"
  but "when the verdicts agree on real material";
- the condition is recorded in advance, or it shifts every time somebody feels
  like showing it;
- narrow publicity is not the same as wide: showing it to ten people for feedback
  can and should happen earlier;
- once the condition is met, publicity is not deferred "just a little longer":
  endless polishing is the same refusal, only polite.

## Where it applies

**Works** for launches, announcements, publications, reaching a new audience.

**Does not work** where feedback is the very point of showing: an early prototype
is shown precisely to learn what is wrong.

**Sign of prematurity:** the most expensive class of defect has never once been
checked on real material.
